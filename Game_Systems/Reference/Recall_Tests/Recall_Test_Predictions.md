# Recall Test — Predictions

**Written before consulting any source.** Committed first so the comparison cannot be adjusted
after the fact. Results go in `Recall_Test_Results.md`.

Purpose: find out empirically where recall of d20 rules is reliable, so the pinning sheet and the
recall-hazards list are built from measured error rather than from self-report about error.

Stated confidence is part of the prediction — being wrong while confident is a different and worse
result than being wrong while hedging.

---

## Claimed solid

**1. Proficiency bonus by level** — confident
Levels 1–4: +2 · 5–8: +3 · 9–12: +4 · 13–16: +5 · 17–20: +6

**2. Ability modifier formula** — confident
`floor((score − 10) / 2)`

**3. Death saves** — confident
At 0 HP, roll d20 at the start of each turn. 10 or higher is a success, 9 or lower a failure.
Three successes stabilises; three failures is death. Natural 20 restores 1 hit point. Natural 1
counts as two failures. Taking damage while at 0 HP causes one failure, two if the damage was a
critical hit. A melee attack from within 5 feet against a downed creature is an automatic
critical hit.

**4. Exhaustion, 2014 rules** — confident
Six levels. 1: disadvantage on ability checks. 2: speed halved. 3: disadvantage on attack rolls
and saving throws. 4: hit point maximum halved. 5: speed reduced to 0. 6: death. Effects are
cumulative. A long rest reduces exhaustion by one level, provided food and drink.

**5. Restrained condition** — confident
Speed becomes 0 and cannot benefit from any bonus to speed. Attack rolls against the creature have
advantage; its own attack rolls have disadvantage. Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.

**6. Rests** — confident on long, less so on short
Long rest: 8 hours, at least 6 of them sleeping with no more than 2 hours of light activity.
Regain all hit points and half the character's total Hit Dice (minimum 1). No more than one per
24 hours. Must begin with at least 1 hit point.
Short rest: at least 1 hour. Spend Hit Dice, rolling each and adding the Constitution modifier.

**7. Difficulty class ladder** — confident
Very easy 5 · Easy 10 · Medium 15 · Hard 20 · Very hard 25 · Nearly impossible 30

## Claimed shaky

**8. Fireball** — moderately confident
3rd-level evocation. Range 150 feet. 20-foot-radius sphere. Dexterity save, 8d6 fire damage, half
on a success. +1d6 per slot level above 3rd. Ignites flammable objects that are not worn or
carried.

**9. Owlbear (CR 3)** — low confidence on exact numbers
AC 13 (natural armour). HP 59 (7d10+21). Speed 40.
Multiattack: one beak, one claws. Beak +7 to hit, 1d10+5 piercing. Claws +7, 2d8+5 slashing.
STR 20, DEX 12, CON 17, INT 3, WIS 12, CHA 7.
Keen Sight and Smell: advantage on Perception checks relying on sight or smell.

**10. Goblin (CR 1/4)** — low confidence on exact numbers
AC 15 (leather armour, shield). HP 7 (2d6). Speed 30.
Scimitar +4 to hit, 1d6+2 slashing. Shortbow +4, 1d6+2 piercing, range 80/320.
Nimble Escape: Disengage or Hide as a bonus action.
STR 8, DEX 14, CON 10, INT 10, WIS 8, CHA 8.

**11. Cure Wounds** — moderately confident
1st-level evocation. Touch. Regain 1d8 + spellcasting ability modifier hit points. +1d8 per slot
above 1st. No effect on undead or constructs.

**12. Magic Missile** — moderately confident
1st-level evocation. Range 120 feet. Three darts, 1d4+1 force damage each, automatically hitting.
One additional dart per slot above 1st.

## Edition hazard — deliberately included as the predicted failure

**13. Exhaustion, 2024 rules** — low confidence, and flagged in advance as the case where
blending is most likely
Believed to have become a single cumulative penalty rather than six distinct effects: roughly
−2 to d20 tests per level and some speed reduction per level, death at 6. Genuinely unsure of the
numbers.

**14. Cure Wounds, 2024** — low confidence
Believed to have been increased to 2d8 base.

# Round 2 — deliberately obscure

Round 1 returned 4/4 exact, but the sample was the most-cited content in the game. This round picks
low-citation entries to find where recall actually degrades. Written before consulting sources.

**15. Otyugh (CR 5)** — low confidence
AC 14 (natural). HP 114 (12d10+48). Speed 30. STR 16, DEX 11, CON 19, INT 6, WIS 13, CHA 6.
Multiattack: one bite and two tentacles. Bite +6, 2d8+3 piercing plus disease (Con save DC 15).
Tentacle +6, reach 10 ft, 1d8+3 bludgeoning plus grapple, escape DC 13. Has a Tentacle Slam
action and Limited Telepathy.

**16. Grick (CR 2)** — low confidence
AC 14 (natural). HP 27 (6d8). Speed 30, climb 30. STR 14, DEX 14, CON 11, INT 3, WIS 14, CHA 5.
Stone Camouflage: advantage on Stealth in rocky terrain. Multiattack: tentacles then beak.
Tentacles +4, 1d6+2 slashing. Beak +4, 1d6+2 piercing.

**17. Glyph of Warding** — low confidence, chosen for parameter density
3rd-level abjuration. Casting time 1 hour. Range touch. Consumes 200 gp of diamond dust.
Duration until dispelled or triggered. Explosive runes: 5d8 damage of a chosen type in a 20-foot
radius, Dexterity save for half. Spell glyph: stores a spell of 3rd level or lower.
+1d8 per slot above 3rd.
